2. Clubs and roles

  • We may suspend accounts that misuse the service or endanger the community.

4. Subscriptions

  • Match Manager is paid for by clubs, never by parents. A club plan covers a season for the whole club and includes a number of player slots; extra slots are sold in packs. The price is shown clearly before a club subscribes.
  • New clubs may try the app first: each team gets a number of free match days before the club chooses a plan.
  • A club can cancel any time; paid seasons already started are not refunded.
  • If a club's plan lapses, staff lose the ability to start new match days and events, but existing data remains visible — we never hold a club's data hostage. Prices may change with reasonable advance notice to the club.
