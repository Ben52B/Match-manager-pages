The live match screen

Set the formation, start the clock, make a change with a tap. Goals, the keeper and half-time are all one touch — no clipboard, no guessing later.

  • Wall-clock timing: leave the pitch, come back, the clock is right.
  • Fixtures come straight from the calendar and start prefilled on the day.
  • Head-to-head with the opponent before you play them.

Play time you can stand behind

Minutes per game for every player, time in goal counted the same as time on the field, missed games never held against anyone. Sort the table, spot who needs minutes, share the season — by league, by cup, or the whole season.

  • Season and competition reports as a page, not a PDF (PDF too, if you want one).
  • Player history a coach can open in a tap.

One hub per team

Messages, photos, polls and reminder cards with a map and one-tap directions. Results post themselves at full time if the coach wants. Reactions, editing, reporting and blocking — the basics done right for a children's community.

What parents see

A parent's app is about their child. Their minutes, their goals, whether they started or came on, the story of each match, and the team's season report. Never another child's numbers.

Private by design

Parents link to their own child with a code from the club. Children never hold accounts. Photo sharing is off until the club turns it on.

  • Availability for games and training in one tap.
  • A private thread with the coaches.
  • Match pages: score, timeline, who scored, halves and substitutions.

Questions

Do parents pay anything?

No. The club subscribes; families join free with a code from the club.

Do children use the app?

No. Accounts are for adults only — coaches, club staff and parents or guardians. A child's details are entered and controlled by their club and their own family.

Does it work at the ground without signal?

Yes. The live match runs on the phone and syncs when you are back online.

Can the club manager see every team?

Yes. The whole club in one view: results, calendar and play-time fairness per team, plus club-wide announcements that reach every family.

What about last season's records?

Bring a season in with one paste of your fixtures and results; the rest is tracked live from your first match day.

How do I get started?

Install from the links above, create your club, add your teams — the guided setup takes a few minutes. Coaches and families join with codes you share.
