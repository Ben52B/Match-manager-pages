The reminder posts itself.

Tomorrow's game is on the calendar, so every family gets a card in the team chat: time, ground, a map and one-tap directions. Nobody had to type it.

  • Works with no signal at the ground.

Questions

Do parents pay anything?

No. The club subscribes; families join free with a code from the club.

Do children use the app?

No. Accounts are for adults only: coaches, club staff and parents or guardians. A child's details are entered and controlled by their club and their own family.

Does it work at the ground without signal?

Yes. The live match runs on the phone and syncs when you are back online.

Can the club manager see every team?

Yes. The whole club in one view, plus club-wide announcements that reach every family.

How do I get started?

Install from the links at the top, create your club, add your teams. The guided setup takes a few minutes. Coaches and families join with codes you share.
